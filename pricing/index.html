Pricing

How RETIS prices its work

Three pricing models across five commercial pillars — fixed price for standardised products, starting-from for services whose scope varies, and custom quote for enterprise engagements. Nothing here is hidden; what varies is explained.

!

Cybersecurity, Secure Software, Digital Growth and Training prices below are positioned against real Kenyan and global market benchmarking — set deliberately competitive against typical local rates. Secure Hosting pricing is still an illustrative placeholder pending RETIS's own infrastructure cost confirmation. All figures remain subject to final confirmation per engagement.

Three Pricing Models

How to read this page

Fixed Price

For easily standardised products — a hosting tier or a training session priced per person. What you see is what you pay.

Starting From

For services where scope genuinely varies by organisation size, systems and assets. The starting figure is real, but your final quote depends on scope agreed during scoping.

Custom Quote

For enterprise engagements where standardising a number would be misleading. We explain what determines the price instead of hiding it.
