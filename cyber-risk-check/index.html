How This Tool Works

Why start with the Free Cyber Risk Check

Most organisations don't know where to start with cybersecurity. This tool gives you a preliminary, educational read on your risk profile in minutes, so your next conversation — whether with RETIS or internally — starts from a clearer picture.

It's preliminary, on purpose

Self-reported answers can't replace a technical assessment. The score is designed to start a conversation, not end one.

It's built for Kenyan organisations

Questions reflect the risk patterns RETIS sees across Kenyan banks, SACCOs, fintechs and enterprises — not a generic global template.

It leads somewhere useful

Every result points toward a concrete next step — a professional Security Gap Assessment, or FINSEC 360™ for financial institutions.
